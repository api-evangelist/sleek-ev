# SLEEK EV

> Electric motorcycles designed for sustainable urban mobility in Southeast Asia.

## About

SLEEK EV is a Thai-Singaporean electric motorcycle company founded in 2022, headquartered in Singapore with primary operations in Thailand. The company's mission is "Sustainability must be affordable" — making EV 2-wheelers accessible for everyday riders. SLEEK EV is BOI-certified and backed by ORZON Ventures (OR + 500 TukTuks), January Capital, A2D Ventures, and Krungsri Finnovate.
